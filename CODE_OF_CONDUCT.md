# Code of conduct

Symbulator has been used by students, teachers and engineers since 1999, in
many countries and many languages. Its spaces (the issue tracker, pull
requests, discussions and any other channel the project runs) should be
welcoming to all of them.

## What is expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are learning. Many
  of Symbulator's users are students meeting circuit analysis for the first
  time.
- Assume good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Keep criticism about the work, specific and constructive.
- Credit other people's work and sources.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or conduct of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this code, and may restrict
participation by anyone who breaks it, temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, write to the maintainer at the address given in
`pyproject.toml`. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
